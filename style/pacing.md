# Pacing

- Total length 32-40 s. 6 scenes, each 4-7 s, except the example demo: 9-11 s.
- One thing moves at a time. Entrances 0.5-0.8 s with ease-out (cubic-bezier .2,.7,.2,1); nothing bounces more than a
  small overshoot.
- Every line of text stays still and fully visible for at least 2.5 s: long enough to read twice.
- Transitions: a slow slide or a shape that carries across the cut. Fades are linear and at least 0.6 s long.
- No flashes: never cut from a light frame to a dark one, never fade a dark panel in fast. Brightness changes gently.
- The camera (zoom/pan on a screenshot) moves one way only, slowly (max ~8% scale over a scene).
- The last scene holds still for at least 1.5 s at the end.
